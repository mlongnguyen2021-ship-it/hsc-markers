# English Standard source catalogue

This library contains 15 unique source PDFs. Every PDF has a same-named Markdown companion for local search. Use the PDF as the source of truth whenever layout, typography, images, handwriting, or OCR accuracy matters.

The supplied 2024 Paper 2 criteria and module-rubric calibration are encoded in the marker's [module rubric reference](../../skills/mark-hsc-english-standard/references/module-rubrics.md). Exact criteria supplied with a question always take priority.

## Conversion notes

- The two supplied copies of `LOTD English Standard Section 1 Texts` were byte-for-byte identical and are stored once.
- Three image-only LOTD PDFs required OCR. Their Markdown companions are search aids and may contain recognition errors.
- No official marking-guide PDF was included in this English collection. Do not imply that the question papers or blank writing booklets contain official sample answers.
