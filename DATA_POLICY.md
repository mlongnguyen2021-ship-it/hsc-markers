# Data and source policy

This repository contains reusable marking logic and a public Biology source library. The contributor has confirmed that the included source files may be used and published here.

Every source PDF is stored with a same-named Markdown companion for search and analysis. Markdown conversions are derivative text and may lose diagrams, formatting or OCR accuracy; the PDF remains the source of truth for visual material.

Do not commit:

- student names, responses, marks, identifiers, or assessment records;
- local file paths, access tokens, credentials, or private links.

Only add papers, marking guidelines, syllabuses, notes or other references when you have permission to publish them. Contributors remain responsible for confirming the rights and licence status of anything they add.

Examples and tests must be newly written, anonymous, and free of copied source passages.
